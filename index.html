EXperimental Thermo-REactive Materials & Environments

Investigating and engineering materials at the extreme.

We study and engineer high-temperature materials for hypersonic flight, atmospheric entry, and energy applications. Our work combines plasma wind tunnel and benchtop reactor experiments, X-ray imaging across scales, and physical models of gas–material interaction.

Research

Ablation · Gas-Material Interaction

Ablation and Gas-Material Interaction

We study how high-temperature materials respond to reactive gas environments — from pyrolysis and surface recession to the fundamental chemistry driving gas–material interaction.

Plasma · Wind Tunnel

Plasma Wind Tunnel Experiments

We conduct experiments in inductively coupled plasma facilities to reproduce the heating of atmospheric entry, and develop methods to reconstruct the flow conditions a sample actually experiences.

Micro · Meso · Multiscale

Multiscale X-ray Imaging

We design in situ and operando experiments that combine X-ray imaging with environmental reactors, to study how material micro- and meso-scale structures evolve under extreme conditions including high temperatures and reactive atmospheres.

Porous Media · Transport

Porous Media and Transport

We study heat and mass transport in porous materials experimentally and computationally — from permeability and conductivity of fibrous insulators to the poromechanical behavior of parachute textiles under atmospheric descent conditions.

Code

Our open-source tools are released through the lab's GitHub organization.

PlasFlowSolver

Data reduction for ICP wind tunnels. Recovers enthalpy, temperature, and velocity from static pressure, stagnation pressure, and stagnation-point wall heat flux, assuming local thermal and chemical equilibrium.

SCAM

(not so) Simple Code for Ablative Materials — a public release of the lab's material response solver.
